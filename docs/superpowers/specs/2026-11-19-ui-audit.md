# TiroAgents desktop UI audit (branch feature/world, worktree C:\Users\ASUS\AgentOfficeWork\world)

Read-only. Nothing in the repo was changed, built or committed. Method: static read of the code, plus live screenshots of the real pages served by `server.mjs` on port 4790 with a temp profile, in an offscreen Electron window (`--mute-audio`, `--disable-features=CalculateNativeWinOcclusion`). Driver scripts: `shoot.cjs` (main run), `shoot2.cjs` (second pass). All processes were killed and temp profile dirs removed.

Abbreviations: `AO.html` = `public/Agent Office.html` (a template block followed by an inline script; lines 1-680 are markup, 680-1227 the component). `OO` = `public/office-objects.js`. `HUD` = `public/hud.js`.

Important limit of the live run: the pages were loaded WITHOUT the desktop preload (`window.agentOfficeDesktop`), so controls that only exist in the packaged app (Claude sign-in/out and API key, music-folder picker, Spotify sign-in, per-CLI "Sign in", Breaker-box notch and Guard rows, "Confirm in TiroAgents") are inventoried from code only and are not in the screenshots. The same missing preload is why some panels show the literal text `null` (see problem 12).

## PART 3 - FINDINGS (most severe first)

1. The town hamburger menu is not a menu: its list is always open. `.hud-menu` sets `display:flex` and only `.hud-panel[hidden],.hud-row[hidden]` get a `[hidden]` override (HUD:45 vs HUD:71). Measured in the live page: `hidden=true`, computed `display:flex`, 204x288 px both at load and after toggling (meta2.json `townMenu_*`; `town-base-lg.png` shows it open on first paint). The toggle only flips `aria-expanded`. Panels then open on top of the permanently visible list (the "Manor" row pokes out behind the Tasks panel in `town-tasks-panel-sm.png`).

2. The same function exists in the office and the town as two different implementations with different looks and different content. Party: office native 340 px panel with presence, Visit and Games (AO.html:219-300) vs HUD Party panel without them (HUD:130-150). Graphics: centred modal with scrim and pixel title font (AO.html:421, `office-graphics-lg.png`) vs side panel in system font (HUD:84, `town-graphics-panel-lg.png`). Tasks: always-open Today board in the office (AO.html:121) vs read-only HUD `Tasks` panel in the town (HUD:78). Build: a toggle pill with hammer icon in the office (AO.html:193) vs a `Build` menu row in the town that navigates to another page (HUD:200). Music/Manor are the HUD panel in both, but open from different places (office hamburger bottom-right, town hamburger top-right).

3. Controls sit in different corners on each surface. Office: settings hamburger bottom-right, header pills top-right, Back-to-town top-left. Town: hamburger top-right, back link top-left. Meeting room: panel bottom-right. Island/tray: separate OS-level places. The user must learn that "settings" is bottom-right in the office and top-right in the town. Related office controls are split across distant corners: Reports/Usage/Awaiting-permission top-right, goal entry top-right popover AND left Today board, Party/Team/Agents/Music/Manor/Claude in bottom-right hamburger but their panels open top-right (opposite end of the screen from the button), Build and Town beside the hamburger but Back-to-town in the top-left card.

4. Panels cover each other, the header and the world, with no common rule. Evidence:
   - HUD panels are fixed at top:68 right:12, which lands on the header's second half: `office-music-lg.png`, `office-manor-lg.png`, `office-claude-lg.png` cut off the right end of the pill row (the `Give Tiro a task` button is half hidden).
   - A HUD panel sits exactly on top of the office Party panel (Party at right:16 top:96 340 px wide is fully inside Music's 1068-1428 x 68-436 box): opening Music with Party open hid Party entirely (`office-overlap-music-party-lg.png`, Party panel not visible although the Party toggle is still "open").
   - The Team panel's bottom edge (876 px) runs under the bottom-right cluster (`-`, Fit, `+`, Town, hamburger at 854-884): `office-team-lg.png` shows the cluster drawn over the Forge card.
   - Opening the meeting-room panel (bottom-right) covers the whole cluster, so Town/Build/hamburger become unreachable until `Leave room`: `scene-meeting-room-joined-lg.png` (cluster absent). Same at 1000x700.
   - Mutual exclusion is inconsistent: `toggleAgents` closes Team and Party (AO.html:1054), `toggleParty` closes only Team (AO.html:1073, so Agents + Party can both be open, Party drawn on top at z 2955 vs 2950), `toggleRoster` closes neither. HUD panels close nothing of the office's, and are z 5200 while `.oo-win` is 5001 and modals 4501.
   - Escape closes Team/desk info but not Party, Agents, Today or the dispatch popover (AO.html:757). During the first capture pass the Party panel stayed open through an Escape and polluted the next shot.
   - The dispatch popover stays open under other windows (seen behind the Reports window in the second pass).
   - Agent drawer at 1440: right 440 px drawer forces the header to wrap and `+ Give Tiro a task` jumps to a second row on top of the Today board (`office-agent-select-lg.png`).
