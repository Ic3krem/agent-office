# Breaker box and subscription guard

Hard switches, and the guard that stops at your plan limit.

(Agent Office v1.2.8, 2026-11-04)

The breaker box in the office holds hard switches. They are real: they gate what Tiro can do.

**Breaker box switches:** Tiro (manager) on/off, Autopilot, Hiring, Terminal approvals, the Subscription guard, and (desktop app only) the Notch, which stops at the 5-hour or weekly plan limit instead of spending extra-usage credits.

Switches are stored in `~/.claude/agent-office/switches.json`. See Configuration and Hiring and approvals.
